\section{Coding construction, numerics, and proofs}\label{app:code}

\subsection{Structural serialisation}\label{app:structural}

Fix the contexts \((C_0,C)\); the grammar catalogue of \(C_0\) contains 17 token values: 16 active fixed-arity operators and one reserved, non-executable schema token. A structure \(T\) with \(n(T)\ge1\) nodes is serialised into three contiguous fields: (i) the Elias-gamma codeword for \(n(T)\) \citep{elias1975codes}; (ii) the \(n(T)\) five-bit tokens of a canonical preorder traversal of \(T\); and (iii) the leaf payloads in traversal order. Boolean payloads occupy one bit, literal numeric constants are canonical IEEE-754 binary64 values, constants fixed in \(C_0\) or \(C\) are omitted from the bitstream, and variable identifiers and basis-list lengths use self-delimiting integer codes. The payload schema, byte order, string encoding, and grammar version are fixed in \(C_0\). Fitted coefficients never appear in the tree serialisation; they enter only through the parameter code \(\ell_{\rm p}(e,q\mid T,C)\).

Let \(\ell_\gamma(n)=2\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor+1\) be the Elias-gamma codeword length for \(n\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\). Because the leaf payload code of each token has a conditional Kraft sum of at most one, relaxing the fixed-arity parse constraints gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:appstructmass}
\sum_{T:n(T)=n}2^{-\ell_{\rm s}(T\mid C)}
\le 2^{-\ell_\gamma(n)}\left(\frac{17}{32}\right)^n.
\end{equation}
Token sequences that fail the parse or violate admissibility only tighten this bound, which therefore holds for every set of grammar-valid structures, including \(\mathcal T_C\) enlarged by the refined record of Section~\ref{sec:isolation}. Since Elias-gamma codewords form a complete prefix code with \(\sum_{n\ge1}2^{-\ell_\gamma(n)}=1\), summing Eq.~\eqref{eq:appstructmass} over \(n\ge1\) gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:appstructkraft}
\sum_{T\in\mathcal T_C}2^{-\ell_{\rm s}(T\mid C)}
\le \sum_{n\ge1}2^{-\ell_\gamma(n)}\left(\frac{17}{32}\right)^n
\le 1.
\end{equation}
The leading Elias-gamma field fixes \(n(T)\), and the token arities and self-delimiting payloads fix every field boundary, so the structural serialisation is prefix-decodable given \(C\).

\subsection{Parameter and response normalisation}\label{app:paramresponse}

\paragraph{Parameter code} The Elias-delta code \citep{elias1975codes} is prefix-free on \(\mathbb Z_{>0}\) with the length \(\ell_\delta(m)\) of Section~\ref{sec:codes} and \(\sum_{m\ge1}2^{-\ell_\delta(m)}\le1\). The sign convention makes \(q_j\mapsto(\mathrm{sign}(q_j),|q_j|+1)\) injective from \(\mathbb Z\) into \(\{0,1\}\times\mathbb Z_{>0}\), so each coordinate codeword satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:appparamkraft}
\sum_{q_j\in\mathbb Z}2^{-(1+\ell_\delta(|q_j|+1))}
\le 2\cdot\frac12\sum_{m\ge1}2^{-\ell_\delta(m)}\le 1.
\end{equation}
Concatenating \(d(T)\) coordinate codewords and prefixing the Elias-gamma codeword for \(e-2\) gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:appparam}
\sum_{e=3}^{9}\sum_{q\in\mathbb Z^{d(T)}}2^{-\ell_{\rm p}(e,q\mid T,C)}\le1.
\end{equation}
The joint Kraft sum over \((e,q)\) factorises as
\begin{equation}\label{eq:appexponent}
\sum_{e=3}^{9}\sum_{q\in\mathbb Z^{d(T)}}2^{-\ell_{\rm p}(e,q\mid T,C)}
=\Big(\sum_{e=3}^{9}2^{-\ell_\gamma(e-2)}\Big)
\prod_{j=1}^{d(T)}\Big(\sum_{q_j\in\mathbb Z}2^{-(1+\ell_\delta(|q_j|+1))}\Big)\le 1.
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Fit-set quantisation bound (Remark~\ref{rem:quantisation})} Let \(S\) be the fit set of a record and \(\|f\|_S=\big(|S|^{-1}\sum_{i\in S}f(x_i)^2\big)^{1/2}\) the empirical root-mean-square norm. After the fit-set standardisation of \(R_C\), every non-constant basis column \(\widetilde\phi_j\) of \(T\) has mean zero and unit root-mean-square norm on \(S\), and the intercept column equals one, so \(\|\widetilde\phi_j\|_S=1\) for \(j<d(T)\). With rounding perturbations \(\delta_j=\widetilde\theta_j-\theta_j\), \(|\delta_j|\le h/2\), Minkowski's inequality gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:appquant}
\Big\|\sum_{j=1}^{d(T)-1}\delta_j\widetilde\phi_j\Big\|_{S}
\le\sum_{j=1}^{d(T)-1}|\delta_j|\,\|\widetilde\phi_j\|_{S}
\le\frac{d(T)\,h}{2}.
\end{equation}
Because \(\delta\) rounds the solved ridge coefficients rather than perturbing the design matrix, Eq.~\eqref{eq:appquant} holds regardless of the condition number of the Gram matrix. With \(\mu\) and \(\widetilde\mu\) the full-precision and quantised fitted values, the triangle inequality \(\|y-\widetilde\mu\|_S\le\|y-\mu\|_S+\|\widetilde\mu-\mu\|_S\) then gives \(\mathrm{RMSE}_{\rm q}\le\mathrm{RMSE}_{\rm fp}+d(T)h/2\), which is at most \((1+\kappa)\,\mathrm{RMSE}_{\rm fp}\) whenever the rule of Remark~\ref{rem:quantisation} finds a qualifying step.

\paragraph{Response code normalisation} For \(0<\rho<1\), the escape mass of Eq.~\eqref{eq:escape} sums to one over \(\mathbb Z\):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:appgeom}
\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}g_\rho(k)
=\frac{1-\rho}{1+\rho}\left(1+2\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\rho^k\right)=1.
\end{equation}
Because the bins \(B_k\) of Eq.~\eqref{eq:bin} partition \(\mathbb R\), the integrated Student-\(t_\nu\) masses satisfy \(\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}p_{m,i}(k\mid x_i,C)=1\) for every decoded record with \(\sigma_i(m)>0\), so the mixture of Eq.~\eqref{eq:binmass} also sums to one, and taking the product over the \(N\) diagnosis observations gives exact normalisation over \(\mathbb Z^N\):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:appresponsevector}
\sum_{\mathbf k\in\mathbb Z^N}\prod_{i=1}^{N}r_{m,i}(k_i\mid x_i,C)=1.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Stable finite-bin evaluation}\label{app:stablebins}

For bin \(B_k=[b_k^-,b_k^+)\), let \(F\) and \(\bar F=1-F\) be the distribution and survival functions of the standardised Student-\(t_\nu\) law, and let \(a=(b_k^--\mu_i(m))/\sigma_i(m)<b=(b_k^+-\mu_i(m))/\sigma_i(m)\). Because the naive difference \(F(b)-F(a)\) suffers catastrophic cancellation in the tails, EIDOS evaluates \(\log p_{m,i}(k\mid x_i,C)\) as \(\log F(b)+\log\{-\operatorname{expm1}(\log F(a)-\log F(b))\}\) in the left tail (\(b<0\)), as \(\log\bar F(a)+\log\{-\operatorname{expm1}(\log\bar F(b)-\log\bar F(a))\}\) in the right tail (\(a>0\)), and directly for bins straddling the origin (\(a\le0\le b\)). The analytic escape log mass \(\log g_\rho(k)=\log(1-\rho)-\log(1+\rho)+|k|\log\rho\) is combined with the Student-\(t_\nu\) log mass by a two-term log-sum-exp.

\subsection{Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:reconstruction}}\label{app:proofreconstruction}

\begin{pf}
(i) By Appendix~\ref{app:structural}, the structural bitstream is prefix-decodable given \(C\) and recovers \(T\) and its parameter dimension \(d(T)\). By Appendix~\ref{app:paramresponse}, the sign-bit and Elias-delta codewords (with the Elias-gamma exponent offset) form a prefix code over \(\{3,\ldots,9\}\times\mathbb Z^{d(T)}\) and recover \((e,q)\). Clause (v) of Definition~\ref{def:eidoscontract} fixes the schema, field order, and byte order, so \(\operatorname{Ser}_{C_0,C}\) is injective and \(\operatorname{Dec}_{C_0,C}\) is its left inverse on all candidate, fold, selection, and refit records.

(ii) \(\ell_{\rm s}(T\mid C)\) depends only on the token sequence and leaf payloads of \(T\), and \(\ell_{\rm p}(e,q\mid T,C)\) only on the indices \(q_j\) and the grid exponent \(e\). Each response codelength \(\ell_{r,i}(m,k_i\mid x_i,C)=-\log_2 r_{m,i}(k_i\mid x_i,C)\) is determined by the decoded parameters \(\widetilde\theta=hq\), the basis standardisation constants of \(R_C\), the covariate vector \(x_i\), the symbol \(k_i\), and the coding constants \((\Delta,o,\nu,\varepsilon,\rho)\) frozen in \((C_0,C)\). The standardisation constants are deterministic functions of \(T\) and the fit-set covariates under \(C_0\), so no untransmitted optimiser state enters any score.

(iii) By clause (iv) of Definition~\ref{def:eidoscontract}, \(A_{\rm disc}\), \(S_C\), and \(R_C\) are deterministic maps seeded from \((C_0,C)\), and by clause (v) \(\operatorname{Ser}_{C_0,C}\) is canonical. Re-executing \(A_{\rm disc}\) and Eq.~\eqref{eq:selectionmap} on the archived construction data and platform therefore reproduces both selected and reported model records, the score ledger, and \(P\) byte for byte.
\end{pf}

\subsection{Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:jointkraft}}\label{app:proofkraft}

\begin{pf}
Substituting Eq.~\eqref{eq:eidoscode} into the sum of Eq.~\eqref{eq:jointkraft} and factorising over the three coding stages gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:appkraftfactor}
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{T\in\mathcal T_C}\sum_{e=3}^{9}\sum_{q\in\mathcal Q_T^+}\sum_{\mathbf k\in\mathbb Z^N}
2^{-L_{\mathrm{EIDOS}}(T,e,q,\mathbf k\mid X,C)}\\
=\sum_{T\in\mathcal T_C}2^{-\ell_{\rm s}(T\mid C)}
\sum_{e=3}^{9}\sum_{q\in\mathcal Q_T^+}2^{-\ell_{\rm p}(e,q\mid T,C)}
\sum_{\mathbf k\in\mathbb Z^N}\prod_{i=1}^{N}r_{m,i}(k_i\mid x_i,C).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
For every admissible triple \((T,e,q)\) with positive decoded scale, the innermost sum equals one by Eq.~\eqref{eq:appresponsevector}. The sum over admissible \((e,q)\) is then at most one by Eq.~\eqref{eq:appparam}, and the sum over \(T\in\mathcal T_C\) is at most one by Eq.~\eqref{eq:appstructkraft}, which establishes Eq.~\eqref{eq:jointkraft}.
\end{pf}

\subsection{Proofs of the evidence-channel propositions}\label{app:proofevidence}\label{app:proofreplacement}\label{app:proofdominance}\label{app:proofconformal}

\begin{pf}[Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:evidence}]
Fix \(D_{\rm disc}\), \(D_{\rm diag}\), and \(C_0\). By Eq.~\eqref{eq:selectionmap} and clauses (iii) and (iv) of Definition~\ref{def:eidoscontract}, \(C=A_{\rm disc}(D_{\rm disc};C_0)\), \((\widehat T,\widehat e_{\rm sel},\widehat q_{\rm sel})=S_C(D_{\rm disc},D_{\rm diag})\), \((\widehat T_{\rm rep},\widehat e_{\rm rep},\widehat q_{\rm rep})=R_C(\widehat T,D_{\rm disc},D_{\rm diag})\), and the score ledger are deterministic functions of \((D_{\rm disc},D_{\rm diag},C_0)\) alone. Replacing \(D_{\rm ver}\), \(D_{\rm cal}\), or \(D_{\rm test}\) leaves every argument of these maps unchanged, and clause (v) ensures that \(\operatorname{Ser}_{C_0,C}\) emits the same byte sequence \(P\).
\end{pf}

\begin{pf}[Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:replacementpower}]
(i) For a compliant pipeline, Proposition~\ref{prop:evidence} gives \(P_j=P\) for every draw \(j\), so \(\Pr(\bigcup_{j=1}^t\{P_j\neq P\})=0\). (ii) Fix all original datasets and condition on \((D_{\rm disc},D_{\rm diag},C_0,C,P)\). Because each rerun starts statelessly from the same frozen context state, \(P_j=\Phi(\widetilde D_j)\) for a fixed deterministic map \(\Phi\). Mutual independence of \(\widetilde D_1,\ldots,\widetilde D_t\) therefore implies conditional independence of the events \(\{P_j\neq P\}_{j=1}^t\), and the probability that no rerun changes the payload is \(\prod_{j=1}^t(1-\pi_j)\), which equals \((1-\pi)^t\) when \(\pi_j=\pi\) for all \(j\). (iii) If \(\Phi(\widetilde D)=P\) almost surely under the replacement distribution, then \(\pi=0\) and \((1-\pi)^t=1\) for all \(t\ge1\).
\end{pf}

\begin{pf}[Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:dominance}]
(i) Under compliance, \(P_j=P\) for every draw \(j\) by Proposition~\ref{prop:evidence}, so \(g(P_j,\widetilde D_j,C)=g(P,\widetilde D_j,C)\) and both detectors have false-alarm probability zero. (ii) Because \(g(\cdot,\widetilde D_j,C)\) is a function of its first argument, \(g(P_j,\widetilde D_j,C)\neq g(P,\widetilde D_j,C)\) implies \(P_j\neq P\); the inclusion holds pointwise on the sample space, so the detection probabilities are ordered for any number of draws. (iii) The disjoint decomposition \(\{P_j\neq P\}=G_j\cup(\{P_j\neq P\}\cap G_j^c)\) proves the stated probability difference. Appending a zero-coefficient basis term or changing only a ledger key preserves evaluated predictions, hence preserves every statistic determined by those predictions.
\end{pf}

\begin{pf}[Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:conformal}]
Conditional on \(\widehat m_{\rm rep}\) and \(C\), let \(s_1,\ldots,s_n\) be the absolute residual scores on \(D_{\rm cal}\) and \(s_{n+1}\) the test score. Under exchangeability of \((s_1,\ldots,s_{n+1})\), the rank of \(s_{n+1}\) among \(\{s_1,\ldots,s_{n+1}\}\) is uniform on \(\{1,\ldots,n+1\}\), with ties broken independently at random. Hence, for \(j=\lceil(n+1)(1-\alpha)\rceil\le n\), \(\Pr(s_{n+1}\le s_{(j)})\ge j/(n+1)\ge 1-\alpha\), and for \(j>n\) the infinite radius gives coverage one \citep{lei2018conformal,barber2023exchangeability}.
\end{pf}

\section{Experimental protocol}\label{app:protocol}

Benchmark family labels follow the PMLB catalogue metadata, and original-source annotations follow the PMLB duplicate-dataset audit.\footnote{PMLB catalogue: \url{https://github.com/EpistasisLab/pmlb}; duplicate-dataset audit with original-source annotations: \url{https://github.com/EpistasisLab/pmlb/issues/167}.}

\subsection{Leakage-detection study}\label{app:leakage}

The suite comprises 15 tasks in three groups of five: (1) known-form physical generators; (2) public-covariate tasks (airfoil self-noise, automobile fuel economy, concrete compressive strength, energy efficiency, and wine quality) with known analytical response functions; and (3) synthetic stress tests (covariate confounder shift, Student-\(t\) heavy-tailed noise, multiplicative heteroscedastic noise, high collinearity, and high-frequency sinusoidal structure). The four replacement strategies define distinct metamorphic relations: bootstrap resampling preserves the empirical support; Gaussian response noise preserves the covariates and approximate conditional means; response permutation preserves the marginals while breaking covariate--response dependence; and paired-row shuffling preserves the empirical joint distribution while changing the summation order. Paired leaking/compliant test-RMSE ratios are aggregated by geometric means over the ten role partitions per task, with 95\% confidence intervals from 5{,}000 cluster-bootstrap resamples over the 15 task clusters.

\subsection{Hyperparameters and development protocol}\label{app:settings}

\paragraph{Tree ensembles and the OpenML PySR budget} LightGBM selects on \(D_{\rm diag}\) over 8 cells: \(300\) or \(1{,}000\) trees, learning rate \(0.03\) or \(0.10\), and maximum leaves \(7\) or \(31\) (minimum child samples \(5\), row subsample \(0.80\), column subsample \(0.90\)). Random forests select over 6 cells: \(500\) trees, feature fraction \(1.00\), \(0.50\), or \(0.33\), and minimum leaf size \(1\) or \(3\). Every cell is fitted on \(D_{\rm disc}\), the cell with the lowest RMSE on \(D_{\rm diag}\) is refitted on \(D_{\rm disc}\cup D_{\rm diag}\), and the refit is evaluated once on \(D_{\rm test}\). All methods run single-threaded with the deterministic task seed. On the OpenML track, PySR runs with 28 iterations, 8 populations, and a \(120\)\,s per-fit timeout instead of the \(200\)\,s budget of Appendix~\ref{app:comparators}.

\paragraph{EIDOS components} Beyond the settings of Section~\ref{sec:eidos}, the pairwise trigonometric family is fitted with ridge penalty \(0.30\); the monomial family includes all features with a strict constant sign on \(D_{\rm disc}\) and instantiates the free, snapped, sparse, and snapped-plus-linear monomials, each fitted with ridge penalty \(10^{-4}\) after normalising positive raw features by their discovery geometric mean; and the sparse-union refit uses a 40-point lasso path. The residual-invariance MMD statistic uses random Fourier features \citep{rahimi2007rff} of the studentised residuals \(z\) with a Gaussian kernel of bandwidth parameter \(1/\{2\,\mathrm{var}(z)\}\) and seed-fixed frequencies; because the frequencies scale as \(1/\mathrm{sd}(z)\), the statistic and its permutation \(p\)-value across the five proxy strata are invariant to rescaling of the residuals.

\paragraph{Nested development protocol} Component design choices were validated before formal testing on nested development frames inside \(D_{\rm disc}\cup D_{\rm diag}\): for each catalogue task, the construction rows were sub-partitioned into five internal development roles with an independent seed, so the formal \(D_{\rm ver}\), \(D_{\rm cal}\), and \(D_{\rm test}\) rows were never accessed during design. Under a pre-declared rule, a component was retained only if it reduced the geometric-mean development RMSE on the tuning subset (every sixth task at offset 0), achieved a ratio below one on the validation subset (offset 1), and remained at or below \(1.020\) on every task family.
